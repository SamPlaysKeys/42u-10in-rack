# 04 — Shelves

Flat mounting surfaces. Prefer one good parametric shelf over a shelf per device.

| Part | Qty | Status | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `shelf-universal-1u` | TBD | Planned | Parametric: depth and vent pattern as parameters. |
| `shelf-universal-2u` | TBD | Planned | For taller gear. |
| `shelf-vented-1u` | TBD | Planned | High airflow variant. |
| `shelf-lipped-1u` | TBD | Planned | Raised lip so small devices do not slide out. |

Design notes:

- A 1U shelf has 43.65 mm of panel to work with and 44.45 mm of space. Anything mounted
  on top of the shelf has to fit in the remaining gap — check the device's own height
  against `u_height` before assuming it fits.
- Weight goes low. A shelf at U40 carrying 3 kg is the fastest way to make this rack tip.
